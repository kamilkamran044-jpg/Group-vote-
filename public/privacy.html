Privacy

Short and plain. Last updated: November 2026.

What we store

  • The poll you create: its title, description and options.
  • Votes: the name each person types and their Yes / Maybe / No answers.

Use a first name or nickname. Please do not type sensitive personal information into polls.

Who can see it

Anyone who has the poll link can see the poll, the names of the people who voted and their answers. Poll links are random and are not listed anywhere or indexed by search engines, but anyone you share the link with can pass it on.

How long we keep it

Polls and their votes are deleted automatically 90 days after they were created.

What we do not do

  • No accounts, no passwords, no email collection.
  • No advertising or tracking cookies, and no analytics scripts on this site.
  • We do not sell or share poll data.

On your device

Your browser remembers the name you last used for voting, so you do not have to type it again. It stays on your device and you can clear it in your browser settings.

Hosting

GroupVote runs on Netlify. Like any web host, Netlify may keep standard technical logs (such as IP address and time of visit) for security and operation. All connections use HTTPS.

Contact

To ask for a poll to be deleted or to raise a concern, write to YOUR-EMAIL@example.com and include the poll link.
