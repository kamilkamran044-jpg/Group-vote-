How it works

From “so, what should we do?” to a clear answer in about a minute.

  1. Create a pollWrite what you are deciding (a restaurant, a date, a trip) and add 2 to 12 options.
  2. Share the linkSend the poll link to your group on WhatsApp, Messenger, email, anywhere. Nobody needs an account.
  3. Everyone votesEach person types their name and marks every option as Yes, Maybe or No. They can come back and change their vote later.
  4. The best option winsThe page ranks the options live and highlights the best choice. If it is a tie, you will see “Tied lead”.

How is the winner chosen?

Yes counts as 2 points, Maybe as 1 point and No as 0. The option with the highest total wins. This rewards choices that most people are happy with, not only the loudest favourite.

Good to know

  • Anyone who has the link can vote, so share it only with people you trust.
  • Voting again with the same name updates that person's earlier vote.
  • Polls are deleted automatically after 90 days.
